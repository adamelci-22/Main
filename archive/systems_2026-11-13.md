# Four-system build, 2026-11-13 (research only, nothing live)

Governor request: (1) the current system tuned to the most robust winner, (2) the current system tuned to the best average month,
(3) three new proxy-to-leveraged-leg systems that could replace FADE and PULLBACK, (4) a stock scanner day-trading system using
signals, charts and news. Champion stays RULEBOOK v5.2.47. Every item below needs a written instruction before it trades.

Data: Massive 5-minute tape 2021-12..2026-10 (6 proxies, 4 PULLBACK legs), Robinhood 5-minute cache 2026-03..10 (8 PULLBACK legs),
Massive gap-scanner set 2025-10-30..2026-11-12 (4,640 stock-days with 5-minute bars, premarket volume, news and sentiment),
Massive 5-minute bars for 9 single stocks 2021-11..2026-11, Massive all-stock daily bars 2023-06..2026-11.
Account model everywhere: 3% risk a trade capped at cash, one position at a time, 3 entries a day, same-system loss block.

## SYSTEM 1: current system, most robust (`tune_book.py`, `results/tune_book_2026-11-13.txt`)
14,244 combinations of the live knobs (FADE floor, lock, early cut, target, chandelier, runaway skip, Friday skip. PULLBACK volume,
minimum stop, opening range, exit).
- Two changes from live: **FADE lock at breakeven** (not +0.15R) at the first +0.5R close. **PULLBACK opening range 9:30-10:00** (not 9:30-9:45).
- Result: worst year -12.6% (live -29.8%), drawdown 30.1% (live 43.3%), 2022-25 +48.6% (live +14.4%). Cost: Robinhood 2026 +124.0% vs +152.2%.
- The in-sample maximin winner (floor 0.3, lock BE, chandelier 3, PULLBACK 100% at the high, range 10:00) has a worst year of -1.2% but earns +6.6, -1.2, -0.8, +4.9 in 2022-25. It avoids losses by not earning. Rejected.
- Warning: walk-forward (pick on the years before, trade the next year) lost to the live rules under all four pick rules: +11.9%, +93.2%, +16.4%, +2.0% vs live +140.7% for 2023-26. Tuning this grid on history does not transfer. The live rules rank in the 92nd percentile on total, 98th on Robinhood 2026, 15th on worst year.
- One change at a time vs live: PULLBACK volume 1.0x helps 4 of 5 years (+24 points total). PULLBACK range 10:00 helps 3 of 5 and cuts drawdown 9 points. Friday FADE trading hurts 5 of 5. Removing the early cut hurts 5 of 5. Floor 0.7 and 1.0 hurt 4 and 5 of 5. The runaway skip cost +29 points in 2022 and helped 2024-26.

## SYSTEM 2: current system, best average month
- FADE: floor 1.0%, lock at breakeven, no early cut, no runaway skip, Friday skip kept. PULLBACK: volume 1.0x, range 10:00, runner on the 20-EMA.
- Average month +2.77% (live +2.03%), winning months 63% (59%), total +314% (live +187%), drawdown 33.8%.
- Cost: Robinhood 2026 +116.3% with a 14.6% drawdown (live +152.2%, 3.5%). 2024 -10.9%.
- Nearest-to-live version: runaway skip off + PULLBACK volume 1.0x + range 10:00. Average month +2.55%, 61% winning, Robinhood 2026 +137.5%.
- Same walk-forward warning as System 1.

## SYSTEM 3: three new proxy-to-leg systems (`new_systems.py`, `new_systems2.py`, `new_systems3.py`)
21 families, 135 settings. Settings chosen on 2021-12..2024-03, judged on 2024-04..2026-10 and Robinhood 2026.
PASS = profit factor >= 1.15 and a positive account in all three blocks.

**A. RELVAL (afternoon relative value). PASS, also the setting H1 chose.**
- 13:30 to 15:30. Sector proxy (SOXX IWM XLK XBI XLF) vs QQQ, both measured from the open.
- Signal: gap >= 1.0% and >= 2.5 standard deviations of the sector's 20-day daily relative move.
- Trade toward QQQ: lagging sector, buy its 3x long leg. Leading sector, buy its inverse leg. Target half the gap. Stop one gap. Out 15:55.
- Blocks: +8.4% PF 1.35 (n 77), +11.9% PF 1.36 (n 109), +8.7% PF 3.06 (n 28). Drawdown 6%. About 40 trades a year. No overlap with FADE or PULLBACK.

**B. DIP (dip-buy in an uptrend). PASS at RSI 10. H1 preferred RSI 20, which stays positive but thin in 2026 (PF 1.11).**
- Known the evening before: proxy closed above its 50-day average and daily RSI(2) < 10.
- Buy the 3x long leg at 9:35. Stop 1% of the proxy (3% on the leg). Out 13:30 (or 15:55).
- Blocks (15:55 exit): +28.6% PF 1.49 (n 56), +67.3% PF 1.68 (n 89), +22.0% PF 1.97 (n 24). 13:30 exit: +14.4%, +61.7%, +21.8%.
- Years (13:30 exit): -4.8, +18.0, +14.9, +1.7, +41.0. Drawdown 16.6%. About 45 signals a year. On a DIP day it takes the morning slot from FADE and PULLBACK.

**C. IBS-TREND (weak close in an uptrend). PASS at 0.1. One of 10 settings passes. Weakest of the three.**
- Proxy above its 50-day average and the prior close in the bottom 10% of the prior day's range. Same entry, stop and exit as DIP.
- Blocks (15:55 exit): +28.4% PF 1.36 (n 73), +56.9% PF 1.42 (n 124), +22.2% PF 1.62 (n 39). Years (13:30 exit): +6.8, +31.4, -2.8, +22.1, +4.1.
- Shares 52 of DIP's 232 signals. Same idea, different trigger.

**As a replacement book (DIP-or-IBS + RELVAL):** positive 4 of 5 years, drawdown 18%, 2022-25 +100% vs live +14%. Robinhood 2026 +41.7% vs live +152.2%.
**As an addition:** SYSTEM 1 + DIP + RELVAL is positive every year, drawdown 21.6%, 2022-25 +118.7%, Robinhood 2026 +149.2%.

Failed (all tested with their source rules): VWAP trend, VWAP pullback, 5-minute ORB, first-hour momentum (earns 2022-23, loses 2026), IBS without the trend filter (earns 2024-26, loses 2022), midday box breakout, FADE on later boxes, prior-day high/low fade, 15:30 rebalance flow, volatility regime switch, breadth thrust, gap-down buy, intraday dip reversal, afternoon fade, RELVAL vs basket, RELVAL vs sibling, N down closes. Breakout and momentum lose in 2024-26. Reversion in the direction of the daily trend is what holds.

## SYSTEM 4: stock scanner. Three designs. None passes.
**Design 1, gap scanner with news ("stocks in play")** (`scan_download.py`, `scan_bt.py`): each day the top 12 gap-ups and top 6 gap-downs by gap/ATR (price >= $5, $30M+ daily volume), with premarket volume, first-bar relative volume, news count and sentiment. Entries: 5-minute ORB, 30-minute ORB, breakout pullback, red-to-green, VWAP hold, FADE long side, high-of-day break, open buy, washout. Long only, cost 0.20%.
- 621 entry-by-filter cells with 40+ trades in each half. 32 had profit factor >= 1.15 in the first half. 2 kept it in the second half. Correlation of first-half and second-half edge across cells: -0.01.
- Gap-ups lose 0.23% a stock-day from the open to 15:55 before costs. News and positive sentiment helped in the first half and hurt in the second.

**Design 2, the live FADE rules on 9 single stocks through 2x funds** (`stock_fade.py`): NVDA TSLA MSTR COIN AMD AMZN AAPL MSFT META.
- Per trade: PF 0.98, 0.66, 1.38 by block. Account alone: -15.7%, -28.0%, +1.3%. Fails.

**Design 3, daily-signal scanner across all liquid stocks** (`scan_dip.py`): DIP, deep DIP, IBS, day-two momentum, buy the open, sell the close, 807 days.
- DIP top 3: -0.16% a trade (PF 0.79) first half, -0.13% (PF 0.87) second half. The proxy DIP edge does not carry to single stocks.
- Day-two momentum top 1: PF 0.99 first half, 1.29 second half. Unconfirmed. The one lead.

Why: the account buys only. Single stocks earn about zero from open to close (-0.03% and +0.03% a day by half). Gap-up names fade. Costs are 3x the leveraged-fund cost. The proxy-to-leg link gives both directions and a cleaner tape, which is why the proxy systems pass and the stock scanner does not.

## Recommendation
1. Do not trade a stock scanner. Evidence against it is consistent across three designs.
2. Best book on paper: SYSTEM 1 + DIP + RELVAL. First step is paper, not money: shadow-log DIP and RELVAL for 30 signals each.
3. SYSTEM 1's two changes are a drawdown trade. They cost about 28 points in a year like 2026 and save about 17 in a year like 2023.
4. Risk on all of it: 135 settings and 14,244 tunes were searched. DIP has 169 trades in five years. Walk-forward says tuned picks do not hold. Size expectations to the 2022-25 column, not the 2026 column.

Instructions that would start each piece: "shadow RELVAL", "shadow DIP", "push system 1", "push system 2", "push RELVAL", "push DIP".
