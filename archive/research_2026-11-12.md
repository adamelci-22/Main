# Research record 2026-11-11 / 2026-11-12 (exits, entries, other systems)

Governor-directed deep dives after the 2026-11-11 LABU FADE trade (peaked +1.56R, closed +0.2R on the +0.15R lock).
Two rule changes came out of it (v5.2.45, v5.2.46). Everything else was tested and **not** adopted. All scripts and
results are in `tools/backtest/` and `tools/backtest/results/*_2026-11-1[12].txt`.

## Method used for every study
- Data blocks: Robinhood cache 2026-03-03..10-14 (the live sample), Massive 5-minute tape split into H1 2021-12..2024-03 and H2 2024-04..2026-10.
- Rules with free choices (thresholds, classes, packages) were chosen on **Massive H1 only** and judged on H2 and Robinhood (never seen).
- PASS = better in every block (account total, or mean R per setup with a paired bootstrap > 90% where stated).

## Adopted
| Version | Change | Key evidence |
|---|---|---|
| v5.2.45 | FADE stop floor 0.5% of the proxy (B2.4) | FADE alone RH +89.3 -> +104.8%, H1 +2.8 -> +32.8%, H2 +24.6 -> +42.1%; every floor 0.3-1.0% wins (plateau). `entry_fade.py`, `entry_fade_floor.py`, `verify_fade_floor.py` |
| v5.2.46 | PULLBACK runner: 1/3 at the morning high, 2/3 to breakeven and a 20-EMA close exit (B3.9, B5.6, C2) | PB alone RH +29.9 -> +38.7%, H1 -19.7 -> -14.1%, H2 +33.9 -> +90.0%; every runner type gives about the same gain. Per setup RH neutral (-0.014R, 41%), H2 strong (+0.145R, 100%). `pb_exit_screen.py`, `pb_exit_confirm.py`, `combo_floor_final.py`, `verify_pb_runner.py` |

Combined account v5.2.46 vs v5.2.44: RH +136.3 -> +162.7% (DD 3.2 -> 4.7%), H1 -14.0 -> +17.3% (DD 30.7 -> 38.8%), H2 +63.8 -> +161.2% (DD 28.0 -> 22.5%).

## Tested and not adopted (counts are rules tested)
- **FADE exits (all failed out of sample):** trails from +0.5R, step locks, 50/50 split, capped trails (`ratchet_screen`, `ratchet_split`); lower targets, scale-outs, time-conditional targets, three-target exits, 1R+2R+runner (`tp_*`); fade-back detectors (`fadeback_screen`); 192 adaptive detector rules, learned 2R classifier (out-of-sample AUC 0.64-0.65), gated rules (overfit: +0.141R in sample, +0.031R and -0.007R out) (`adaptive_exit`); 30 setup-conditional policies (`fade_setup_exits`); lock/cut recheck (split verdict, live lock kept) (`fade_lock_recheck`); on the floored system 47 fixed exits, 20 lock settings, 12 cuts, 29 policies, 180 detectors (`fade_exit_floor`). Only pass: breakeven lock at +0.5R (+6 to +7.5 points, bootstrap 61-72%, single peak, win rate 71 -> 62%, near-zero exits push the A1.1 lockout) -> **not adopted**.
- **Variable exits on v5.2.46 (`variable_exits`):** FADE 43 entry / 28 during / 43 both, PULLBACK 45 / 18 / 46. 0 passed. Largest near misses are about 0.01R per trade.
- **FADE entries (`entry_fade`):** 21 mechanics (box 3/4 bars, break window, retest wait, tolerance, any-touch entry, minimum wait, ATR stops, sides) and 13 entry-time filters: only the stop floor passed.
- **PULLBACK entries (`entry_pb`):** 27 signal changes and 12 filters: 0 passed. PULLBACK loses on H1 under every entry.
- **Other systems (`other_systems`):** 30-min ORB continuation, noise-boundary momentum (Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon 2024), VWAP reversion, gap fade, first-hour momentum. None beats the live book in 2026. First-hour momentum earns on H1/H2 (+53% / +23%) and loses in 2026 (-18%): the mirror of FADE/PULLBACK.
- **Regime rotation (`regime_rotation`):** trailing 10-60 day switch between the live book and first-hour momentum: unstable across lookbacks, not adopted. 50/50 split cuts 2026 from +136% to +41%.

## Limits of this evidence (governor asked "are you fully confident?" — answer: no)
1. Small samples: 63 FADE / 68 PULLBACK trades in the Robinhood window; a real +0.05R/trade gain is invisible at that size.
2. Regime dependence: FADE ~flat and PULLBACK negative on 2021-2024; the rules fit the 2026 market.
3. Multiple testing: about 900 rules tested; the design/holdout split reduces but does not remove lucky passes.
4. Idealised simulation: legs = 3x proxy, fills at the stop/target, simple fees; thin legs (TECS, LABU, SSG) and gaps make live results worse.
5. Families not tested: sizing by setup quality, other universes, trading after 13:30, order-book data, adding more systems.
6. The two adopted changes are unproven live.
