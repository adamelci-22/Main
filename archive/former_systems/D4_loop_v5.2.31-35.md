# Retired D4 text (RULEBOOK v5.2.31 to v5.2.35, replaced by the governor cycle in v5.2.36)

Nothing here is in force as a decision rule; the tools it names stay as information.

**D4. Self-improvement loop (v5.2.31).** `tools/live/improve.py` shadow-runs the version in force against every candidate on each close, at no risk and with no orders: each of the twelve tunables in `tools/live/tunables.json` one step either way (B2.3, B2.5, B3.2, B3.7, B4.1, B5.4) and the structural ideas in `tools/live/ideas.json`. B2.5, B3.7, B4.1 and B5.2 read their numbers from the version in force; the values in those rules are the v1 defaults. Evidence counts only days on or after `policy.shadow_from` in `tunables.json`, so it is out-of-sample by construction. A candidate is READY only when it differed from the version in force on at least 20 days, its paired-bootstrap lower bound (adjusted for the number of candidates ever tracked) is above zero and it gained at least 0.5% of the account. A candidate that is clearly harming, or still undecided after 60 shadow days, is retired into `tools/live/research_ledger.json`, which also holds every earlier study. The real account and the paper model change only when the governor runs `improve.py approve` and then `promote --effective <future trading day>` on a READY numeric candidate, and the promotion is recorded in the commit with the governor's words. An idea (a structural change) that becomes READY is reported to the governor and needs a normal rulebook change (D3). One challenger at a time. Nothing in A1 is tunable. `tunables.json`, `ideas.json`, `research_ledger.json`, `archive/improve/` and the version bump are committed together.

**D4 watchlist (v5.2.32).** `tools/live/watchlist.json` lists tickers and proxies that are only tried in shadow, never traded: a FADE proxy with its two leveraged legs (`add.fade.<proxy>`) or a PULLBACK leg (`add.pb.<leg>`). Each is a candidate like any other and passes the same gate, so each one added raises the Bonferroni burden; keep the list short. `improve.py screen` rates liquidity (median 5-minute bar of at least $50,000, 120 sessions, at most 10% empty bars) and `improve.py watch-add` refuses a symbol that fails it. A TICKER READY line means the evidence is clear; trading it needs a rulebook change by the governor (D3) following `improve.py add-checklist <id>`: tradability and spread, A1.7 and A1.9, `FADE_ROWS`, `PB_LIST`, `PB_PROXY` and `EXPECTED` in `paper.py`, the B lists and C1.2, the neutrality test, the artifact.

**D4 risk, regime and defensive review (v5.2.33).** Reports only; none of it changes an order, a rule or a tunable.
- **Risk as the account lives it.** `improve.py risk` bootstraps a 250-day year from the replayed daily returns (4,000 paths; a path ends at the A1.13 −15% stop) in three scenarios: as replayed, wins 40% smaller, and wins 40% smaller with losses 25% larger (the stand-in for the live-versus-backtest gap). It reports the chance of the hard stop, the median and 5th-percentile year and the drawdowns, and the real account's own record once it has 20 trading days. **A candidate that passes the evidence gate is also risk-checked** on the stressed scenario over the full dataset: its chance of the hard stop may be at most `risk_pstop_slack` (1.0) points above the version in force and its median worst drawdown at most `risk_dd_slack` (1.0) points above, or it is BLOCKED BY RISK and not READY. The edge must come from the forward shadow; the risk check only stops a candidate that buys return with stop risk.
- **Regime signal.** `improve.py regime` (printed at every close) takes the version in force's own trades on the most recent sessions of the dataset (it grows by one live session a day): +2R reach rate and profit factor of the last 20 model FADE trades, and the profit factor of the last 20 PULLBACK trades, with the C5 bands. Any measure in the bottom band = HOSTILE; in the middle band = THIN.
- **Attribution.** `improve.py attrib [--since D]` splits each real trade's gap to the model (% of position) into entry price, governor hand close and exit/path, and lists model trades the account missed and real trades the model did not have. Printed on Fridays for the week.
- **Defensive review.** When the regime is HOSTILE or the day's health row (C5) has any ACT, `tick` prints DEFENSIVE REVIEW with `improve.py defensive`: each lower `risk.pct` and `size.cap_pct` step, one loss ends the day, and dropping each FADE proxy, scored on the last 60 sessions in the stressed scenario. Report it to the governor at once; the governor decides (a size cut is `menu --risk-off` then the normal approve and promote, a proxy drop is a rulebook change).

**D4 edge protection (v5.2.34).** The regime line carries an edge monitor (last 40 sessions of the version in force: INTACT, THINNING or FADING; FADING = HOSTILE = DEFENSIVE REVIEW). Candidates ruled out by the whole dataset are parked by `improve.py prune` (run it after every promotion or rollback, and when ideas or watchlist entries are added), so evidence is not diluted. After a promotion, `rollback.v<old>` is shadowed at once; ROLLBACK RECOMMENDED goes to the top of the report and to the governor.

**D4 defensive recommendations (v5.2.35, governor 2026-10-28: "isn't this entire self improvement meant to find stuff like this").** At every close `tick` runs the defensive review. When the version in force's chance of the −15% hard stop (stressed scenario, last 60 sessions) is above `risk_target_pstop` (2%), it prints DEFENSIVE REVIEW and a DEFENSIVE RECOMMENDATION: the lower `risk.pct` or `size.cap_pct` with the best whole-dataset %/day among those that bring the stop risk to 2% or below while keeping at least 90% (`risk_cost_max`) of the %/day. The session files it with `improve.py defend <variable> <value> "why"` (a pending proposal judged on risk, not on forward shadow) and reports it; the governor approves and promotes as usual. A promoted defensive change is not shadow-rolled back on return; when the edge monitor is INTACT again `tick` prints RESTORE POSSIBLE and the governor may roll it back.

**D4 session rules.** The checkpoint session never runs `approve`, `promote`, `discard`, `rollback` or `reject`, and never edits `tools/live/tunables.json` by hand, unless the governor gives that exact instruction in writing in the chat. The session may run `improve.py screen` and then `improve.py watch-add` to put a ticker or proxy on the watchlist (shadow only, no order, no change to any traded list); it never adds one to `paper.py`'s traded lists or the B lists without the governor's written instruction (D3). If `tick` prints EVIDENCE READY, IDEA READY, TICKER READY, ROLLBACK RECOMMENDED, DEFENSIVE REVIEW, DEFENSIVE RECOMMENDATION, RESTORE POSSIBLE or RETIRED, or a proposal is pending or approved, put it at the top of the 13:30 report and tell the governor at once (a push notification if the tool exists); never wait silently. On EVIDENCE READY: read `improve.py prompt`, run `improve.py submit` with the READY id or `hold` (hold unless the evidence is clear and fits the recent trades), report it and wait.
