## Tested and not adopted
- Daily-trend filter on FADE (with or against the trend, 10/20/50 sessions): no version beats live FADE on the train years.
- PULLBACK only when the underlying is below its average: lifts 2023 from -18.5% to between -2.7% and -8.0% and cuts drawdown, but trails live PULLBACK on train total. In the book it gives +53.8, +9.7, +8.2, +59.4, +91.9. A lead, not approved by the train rule.
- IBS-TREND threshold 0.05 or 0.15, average 10 or 30 sessions, stop 1.5%, 2.5% or none: the pushed 0.10 / 20 / 1% is the train pick. 34 of 36 settings win both test years. 9 of 36 win all five years.
- IBS-TREND afternoon only (13:30 entry): +1.8% average. The edge is in the morning.
- Dropping FADE: lowers 2026 from +126.8% to +50.5%. Kept.

## Risks, stated plainly
- The mirror alone lost 17.6% in 2024 and earned +59.8% in 2022. It is a bear-market earner. In a year like 2024 it costs the book about 19 points.
- 157 mirror signals in five years. 305 long signals. One search of 36 settings and one of 12 sit on top of yesterday's 135.
- Both sides size at full cash on a 3x leg with a 3% stop. A stop-out costs about 3% of the account and ends that day's entries (A1.2).
- The live code was replayed on the tape and matches the backtest within 1 point a year.

## FADE, stated plainly
- FADE's rules were designed on 2026 data. 2026 is in-sample for it. On the four years it never saw it made +1.0, -10.0, -0.1, -2.0 alone.
- Inside the joint book it adds 0.0, -1.3, -3.4, +3.4 points in 2022-25 and +35.8 in 2026. Removing it changes the 2022-25 average by +0.9 and 2026 by -83.
- It is a cheap option on a 2026-type market, not a proven edge. Its health rule decides: profit factor of the last 30 live trades under 1.0 turns it off.
